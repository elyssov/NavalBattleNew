# Usagi CIC Visual Canon

Status: locked as the primary visual direction for Naval Battle.

## Command cabin

- The flagship bridge is wide and panoramic.
- The forward glazing remains open and readable; it is not covered by a large desk.
- Red-lit apparatus stays on the far side bulkheads.
- One detached landscape MFD is centered in the cabin. The game is played on this MFD.
- The MFD is a broad rectangular operator screen with room for two tactical views.

## Tactical screens

- The MFD contains two square black-green radar screens.
- The hidden source grid remains square, but the player sees circular range rings, bearing spokes, cardinal marks, and a moving sweep line.
- Ships are contact ticks, not painted ship icons.
- Friendly contacts are bright ticks with stable digital number tags.
- Enemy contacts are unlabeled unknown ticks. Enemy names and classes are never printed on the radar.
- Hits, misses, stale contacts, and sunk contacts use separate restrained marks.
- The radar remains interactive: selection, movement, targeting, sonar, and fire must continue to use the same underlying field coordinates.

## Nuclear view

- The CIC nuclear plate uses the same wide cabin and landscape MFD composition.
- Exactly one mushroom cloud appears outside on the left side.
- Burning enemy ships appear outside on the right side.
- The mushroom and ships share a believable mid-distance horizon and atmospheric scale.
- No tsunami, second mushroom, or giant event that washes away the fleet.

## Rejected direction

The grey debug board, text-only tactical canvas, and flat wall-mounted monitor are not owner-facing art. They may exist only as development fallbacks while the Usagi CIC contract is active.
